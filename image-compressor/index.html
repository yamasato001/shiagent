Designed for line art

Keep every fine line.

Heavy compression can lose anti-aliased grays and very thin strokes. Line Art mode selects settings that protect fine lines, while PNG and WebP transparency remain intact.

Everything runs in your browser. PNG uses OxiPNG optimization, while JPEG and WebP are re-encoded at a quality chosen for the selected mode.

FAQ

Common questions

Are my files uploaded?＋

No. Reading, analysis, compression and ZIP creation all happen inside your browser.

Are formats detected automatically?＋

Yes. The tool inspects the actual file data, keeps PNG, JPEG and WebP in their original formats, and accepts all three in the same batch.

How is Exact different?＋

Exact losslessly optimizes PNGs and leaves JPEG and WebP unchanged. Other modes re-encode image data and may change pixel values.

Is there a file limit?＋

SHIAGENT imposes no count limit. Images are processed one at a time. Practical capacity depends on your device memory and browser.
