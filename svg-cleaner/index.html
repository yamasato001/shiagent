FAQ

Common questions

Are my files uploaded?＋

No. SVG parsing and cleanup happen entirely inside your browser.

Will my SVG look different?＋

Path shapes, colors, stroke widths, viewBox and everything else needed for drawing are kept. You can compare before and after previews before saving.

What is removed?＋

Metadata, comments, attributes specific to editors such as Inkscape, excess decimal precision and whitespace, and unsafe attributes such as scripts.

Can I still open the file in my editor?＋

Yes. However, editor-specific information such as layer names may be lost, so keep your original file for further editing.
