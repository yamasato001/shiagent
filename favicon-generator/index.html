FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which sizes are generated?＋

16, 32 and 48px favicon PNGs, a 180px Apple Touch Icon, 192 and 512px PWA icons, and 192 and 512px maskable icons.

What is inside favicon.ico?＋

It contains the 16, 32 and 48px PNG images in one ICO file.

What is a maskable icon?＋

It keeps important artwork in a safe zone so Android and other platforms can crop it into circles or rounded squares without losing content.

Is my image uploaded?＋

No. Resizing, ICO, manifest and ZIP generation all happen inside your browser.
