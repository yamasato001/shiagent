FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I add 10% padding around an image?＋

Yes. Choose Add padding and enter 10%. Horizontal padding uses image width, and vertical padding uses image height.

Can I center an image on a 512×512 canvas?＋

Yes. Choose Place on a canvas and enter 512 for both dimensions. Larger images are scaled down proportionally to fit.

Can the background be transparent?＋

Yes. Choose PNG or WebP output to preserve transparency. White, black, and custom colors are also supported.

Are images uploaded?＋

No. Reading, padding, and ZIP creation all happen inside your browser.
