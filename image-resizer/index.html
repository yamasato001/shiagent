FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I resize without changing the aspect ratio?＋

Yes. Keep “Preserve aspect ratio” on to fit each image inside the width and height you enter.

Can I resize to 50% of the original?＋

Yes. Choose Percentage and enter 50. Each image’s width and height will be halved from its own original size.

Can I resize HEIC images?＋

Yes. When Original format is selected, HEIC, HEIF and other formats that browsers cannot export are saved as JPEG.

Are images uploaded?＋

No. Reading, resizing and ZIP creation all happen inside your browser.
