POTRACE

Single-color vectors for line art.

Great for black-on-white teaching materials, icons and stamps. It does not preserve the colors of photos or multi-color illustrations.

Conversion, splitting and saving all happen on your device.

FAQ

Common questions

Are my files uploaded?＋

No. Splitting, vectorizing and SVG export all happen inside your browser.

Can I convert photos or color illustrations?＋

You can, but the result is a single-color (black) line-art SVG; original colors and gradients are not kept. It works best for line art on white, icons and stamps.

What is the difference between “Split into many” and “One SVG per image”?＋

Split into many turns each illustration separated by white space into its own SVG. One SVG per image keeps the whole image, including detached parts, in a single SVG.

Lines look jagged or specks remain＋

Choose Smooth to even out wobbly or jagged lines, or Strong to remove small noise.

How is the output size decided?＋

The artwork is centered on a 256, 512 or 1024 square canvas, keeping its aspect ratio.
