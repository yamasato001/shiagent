FAQ

Common questions

Can I add iPhone HEIC photos directly?＋

Yes. Add HEIC/HEIF files by selecting or dragging them in, and convert them to PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Are my images uploaded?＋

No. Reading, converting (including the dedicated HEIC and TIFF decoders) and ZIP creation all happen inside your browser.

What happens to transparency?＋

PNG and WebP keep transparency. Transparent areas become white when an image is converted to JPEG.
