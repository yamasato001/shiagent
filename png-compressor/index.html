FAQ

Common questions

Are my files uploaded?＋

No. Reading, analysis, compression and ZIP creation all happen inside your browser.

How is Exact different?＋

Exact optimizes the original PNG directly without changing pixel values. Balanced, Smallest and Line Art may reorganize color values for their stated goal.

Is there a file limit?＋

SHIAGENT imposes no count limit. Images are processed one at a time, and large images automatically use a memory-bounded path. Practical capacity depends on your device memory and browser.
