FAQ

Common questions

Are my files uploaded?＋

No. Detection, cropping, PNG export and ZIP creation all happen inside your browser.

What kind of images work best?＋

Sheets with several illustrations placed apart from each other on a white background. It cannot recognize subjects in photos or separate overlapping illustrations.

The split is not right. What can I do?＋

Use Fine when illustrations are close together, and Grouped when one illustration is split into several pieces. Check the detected areas preview to see the result.

Can I use transparent PNGs?＋

Yes. Transparent areas are treated as white, so the cut-out PNGs have a white background.

What format and padding do the results have?＋

They are PNG files with 16 px of padding around each detected area.
