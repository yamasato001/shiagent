FLAT BACKGROUND

Made for solid-color backgrounds.

Works best for line art on white paper, illustrations on a plain background, product photos shot on a backdrop, and screenshots. Busy photographic backgrounds, or subjects whose edges share the background color, may not come out cleanly.

No AI model is used: everything runs on your device using color distance and connectivity. HEIC and TIFF are read with a bundled decoder.

FAQ

Common questions

Are my images uploaded?＋

No. Decoding, background removal, export and ZIP creation all happen inside your browser.

What is the difference between “Outer background” and “Every matching color”?＋

Outer background only removes background that is connected to the image edges, so white areas inside the subject, such as eyes or clothing, are kept. Every matching color removes all similar colors, including areas enclosed by lines.

A white fringe remains around the edges＋

Increase edge smoothing or widen the tolerance a little. For noisy JPEGs, a wider tolerance usually helps.

Why can’t I save as JPEG?＋

JPEG cannot store transparency, so transparent results are saved as PNG. Choose a background color to enable JPEG.
