FAQ

Common questions

Are PDFs uploaded?＋

No. Everything from margin detection through ZIP creation runs in your browser.

Are multiple PDFs merged?＋

No. Each PDF is finished separately and saved with a sequential name. Multiple outputs are bundled in a ZIP.

Is orientation detection always exact?＋

It uses text information and page pixels, but sparse or unusual pages may still be misdetected.

Is the original PDF changed?＋

No. The result is saved as a new PDF.
