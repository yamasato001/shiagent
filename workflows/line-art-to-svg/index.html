FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does no white fill produce?＋

A line-only SVG with a transparent background.

What is the difference between automatic and manual?＋

Automatic fills all detected closed areas. Manual lets you add guide lines and exclude areas in the dedicated editor.

What does background processing do?＋

It estimates the border color and removes the connected outer background while keeping enclosed areas intact.

Are files uploaded?＋

No. Trimming, vectorization, white fill, cleanup and ZIP creation all happen in your browser.
