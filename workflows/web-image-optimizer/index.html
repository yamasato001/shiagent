FAQ

Common questions

Are my images uploaded?＋

No. Everything from Crop through ZIP creation runs inside your browser.

What is the Blog preset for?＋

It suits article photos, screenshots and diagrams, converting them to 1200px WebP files.

Does Original size really skip resizing?＋

Yes. Unless Auto Trim is enabled, it keeps the pixel dimensions and only compresses and removes metadata.

How are output files named?＋

With Rename off, “-web” is added to the original name. Turn it on to use a base name and sequential number of your choice.
