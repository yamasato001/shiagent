FAQ

Common questions

Are PDFs or images uploaded?＋

No. Orientation, cropping, OCR, compression and ZIP creation all run in your browser.

What happens when I select multiple images?＋

They are combined in selection order into one searchable PDF. PDF files remain separate outputs.

Are the original files changed?＋

No. Each result is saved as a new PDF.
