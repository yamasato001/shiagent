FAQ

Common questions

Are my images uploaded?＋

No. Everything from loading through compression and ZIP creation runs inside your browser.

Can I process 500 images at once?＋

There is no fixed file-count limit. Capacity depends on your device memory and source dimensions, so splitting extremely large images into smaller batches may be more stable.

Can white and transparent backgrounds be mixed?＋

Yes. Auto detect checks the outer edge of each image before trimming.

Will every output have the same size?＋

Yes. The subject keeps its aspect ratio and is centered on the exact canvas dimensions.

What happens to transparency in JPEG?＋

JPEG does not support transparency, so transparent output is automatically filled white.
