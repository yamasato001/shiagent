About SHIAGENT

A practical toolbox for taking images, SVGs and PDFs from source material to usable output.

Last updated: September 29, 2026

What SHIAGENT is

SHIAGENT is a collection of focused browser tools for repetitive production work: compression, conversion, resizing, cropping, vectorization, SVG cleanup and PDF organization. The goal is not to hide decisions. It is to make each decision visible, repeatable and reversible.

Local first

File processing runs on your device unless a page clearly says otherwise. This reduces unnecessary file transfer and lets compatible results move between tools through the browser's Work Tray.

Design principles

Focused

One clear job per tool.

Inspectable

Preview and compare before saving.

Portable

Standard files out, without account lock-in.

Honest

Limitations and known issues are published.

Project stage

SHIAGENT is under active development. Core image, SVG and PDF tools are available; the local AI line-art experiment remains on hold because its output quality and general-PC requirements do not yet meet the project's standard.
