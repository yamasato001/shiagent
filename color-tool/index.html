FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Tolerance ±10 mean?＋

Colors within ±10 of each RGB channel are treated as a match. This helps with slightly off-white JPEG backgrounds.

Can I make only white transparent?＋

Yes. Use the White to transparent preset and adjust Tolerance if needed.

Do SVG files stay vector-based?＋

Yes. The tool updates fill, stroke, gradient, and CSS color declarations and saves an SVG.

Are files uploaded?＋

No. Color processing and ZIP creation happen entirely inside your browser.
