FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does DPI equivalent mean?＋

Output pixels are calculated from the SVG standard of 96 dpi. A 96px-wide SVG becomes 300px wide at 300 dpi equivalent. PNG output also stores physical resolution metadata.

Can I keep a transparent background?＋

Yes. Choose transparent, white, black or a custom color.

Can multiple SVGs use the same width?＋

Yes. Set a bounding width and height to fit each SVG within the same area while preserving its aspect ratio.

Are files uploaded?＋

No. SVG parsing, rendering and ZIP creation happen inside your browser.
