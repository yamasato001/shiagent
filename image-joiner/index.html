FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I arrange four images in a 2×2 grid?＋

Yes. Choose Grid and set the column count to 2.

Can I join images with different sizes?＋

Yes. Horizontal and vertical layouts center the shorter axis, while grid layouts center each image inside its cell.

Can I use HEIC images?＋

Yes. HEIC/HEIF and other common image formats are decoded locally in your browser.

Are images uploaded?＋

No. Reading, joining, and saving all happen inside your browser.
