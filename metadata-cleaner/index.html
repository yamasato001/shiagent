FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What information is removed?＋

JPEG EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC, and comments; PNG EXIF, text, and timestamps; and WebP EXIF and XMP.

Will image quality change?＋

No. The image pixels are not re-encoded; only metadata sections are removed.

Are color profiles removed?＋

No. ICC color profiles and other information required for correct display are preserved.

Are images uploaded?＋

No. Reading, cleaning, and ZIP creation all happen inside your browser.
