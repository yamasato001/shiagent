HOW IT WORKS

Render. Find closed areas. Lay white underneath.

01

Render the SVG

The SVG is rendered at high resolution on a transparent background, and its lines are read as barriers.

02

Detect closed areas

Transparent areas that cannot be reached from outside are picked as areas to fill.

03

Add white paths

White vector paths are inserted behind the original line art.

MATOPURI WHITE FILL

Add white without touching the lines.

The drawing elements of the original SVG are left as they are; detected fills are added as a dedicated group.

Areas with large gaps in their outline cannot be detected as closed.

FAQ

Common questions

Are my files uploaded?＋

No. Rendering the SVG, detecting closed areas and adding white paths all happen inside your browser.

Is my original line art changed?＋

No. The white fill is added as its own group behind the line art. Processing an SVG that already has a white fill replaces the previous fill.

Some areas are not filled＋

Areas whose outline has a gap are not detected as closed. Standard and Precise bridge small gaps; for larger gaps, draw guide lines in Manual SVG White Fill.

What is the difference between Strict, Standard and Precise?＋

Strict fills only fully closed areas. Standard and Precise render at a higher resolution and bridge small breaks while detecting.
