FAQ

Common questions

Are my files uploaded?＋

No. Editing, recalculating the white fill and saving the SVG all happen inside your browser.

When should I use this editor?＋

When the Automatic SVG White Fill leaves areas unfilled because of gaps in the lines, or fills areas you want to keep transparent.

How do I close a gap?＋

Choose “Close gaps” and click both ends of the gap to draw a guide line. Guide lines are only used to calculate the fill; they are not saved as lines in the SVG.

When are edits applied?＋

The white fill is generated right after the SVG loads and recalculated automatically after every guide line, exclusion, erase or undo.

Can I adjust several SVGs in a row?＋

Yes. Switch files with Previous and Next, and use “Finalize and next” to confirm the current SVG and move on.
