Privacy Policy

SHIAGENT processes files on your device wherever possible. This Policy explains what information the Site handles and where it is stored.

Effective: September 29, 2026Last updated: September 30, 2026

Our approach

SHIAGENT (the “Site”) limits information handling to what is needed to provide the service and protects it under applicable law. Unless a page clearly says otherwise, file processing runs in your browser.

Information handled

Files you processImages, SVGs, PDFs and other files you select, plus their processed outputs.
Browser settingsLanguage, image and PDF work trays, saved custom workflows and tool settings.
Access dataThe delivery server or hosting provider may log IP address, browser and device information, access time, referrer, requested URL and error data.
Contact dataReply-to email, inquiry category, selected tool, message, and any browser, OS, page and error diagnostics you explicitly choose to include.

Local file processing

Unless a page clearly identifies cloud use, files loaded into the Site's tools are not uploaded to the operator's servers. Conversion, analysis, compression, editing and ZIP creation run in your browser.

Files placed in work trays may be stored in IndexedDB in your browser. They may remain available to this Site in the same browser until you clear the tray or delete the Site's browser data.

Purposes

  • Provide Site features and preserve settings you choose.
  • Investigate errors, improve quality, secure the Site and prevent abuse.
  • Understand access patterns and deliver the Site reliably.
  • Respond to inquiries and provide necessary communications.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Cookies and browser storage

The Site uses localStorage for language preferences and custom workflows, IndexedDB for image and PDF work trays, and the current tab's sessionStorage for up to 30 diagnostic events that you may review and optionally submit with an inquiry.

The Site's code does not include cookies for behavioral advertising. Diagnostics are not sent automatically and leave your browser only when you explicitly include them in the contact form.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

To improve the Site, we use Google Analytics, a third-party analytics service provided by Google LLC. It uses cookies and sends Google information such as the address of the page you viewed (without anything after “?”), the referrer, your browser and device type, approximate region and IP address. Files you load into the tools, their contents and their names are never sent. Advertising features (Google signals and ad personalization) are turned off.

On English pages, analytics runs only after you choose “Allow” in the on-screen notice. On Japanese pages it runs unless you turn it off. Information sent to Google is handled under Google's privacy policy.

You can change the analytics setting with the button below.

Sharing and processors

The operator does not sell personal information. Personal information is not disclosed without consent except where permitted or required by law.

The contact form uses FormSubmit to deliver submitted information to the operator's Yahoo! JAPAN email address. The form does not accept image, SVG, PDF or other file attachments. Hosting providers may also process access logs to deliver and secure the Site.

Retention and deletion

Browser-stored information can be removed using a tool's clear control, a tray's delete control or your browser's site-data settings. Session diagnostics are removed when the tab closes. Server logs follow the deployed hosting configuration. Inquiry information is normally deleted within one year after the inquiry is resolved, unless a longer period is needed for law, rights protection or dispute handling.

Security

The operator uses safeguards appropriate to the nature and scale of the information, including access controls, software updates and provider review. No internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your requests

Where the operator holds personal data about you, requests for access, correction, restriction or deletion will be handled after identity verification and in accordance with applicable law. Files and settings stored only in your browser and never received by the operator must be removed by you through the browser.

Children

Minors should obtain parental or guardian consent where required. Parental review is also recommended before a minor sends personal information through any future contact channel.

Changes

This Policy may be updated when the Site, law or information practices change. Material changes will be announced clearly on the Site.

Operator and contact

For privacy, access, correction, restriction or deletion requests, use the contact form or email yamamotoshiki@yahoo.co.jp.
